# Algebraic Operations

## Part 1: Multi-Step Equations & Isolating Variables

### Core Execution Rules

To isolate a variable $$x$$, apply inverse operations in reverse order of operations:

- **Clear fractions:** multiply every term on both sides by the Least Common Denominator (LCD).
- **Expand & group:** use the distributive property to eliminate parentheses, distributing negative signs through the terms.
- **Combine like terms:** consolidate constant terms and variable terms on each side of the equation independently.
- **Isolate variable terms:** add or subtract variable terms so that $$x$$ appears on only one side.
- **Solve:** undo multiplication or division to solve for $$x$$.

### Worked Reference Example

Solve for $$x$$:

$$
\frac{3(x-2)}{4} - \frac{x+1}{3} = 1
$$

**1. Clear fractions by multiplying every term by the LCD (12):**

$$
12 \cdot \frac{3(x-2)}{4} - 12 \cdot \frac{x+1}{3} = 12 \cdot 1
$$

$$
9(x-2) - 4(x+1) = 12
$$

**2. Expand expressions:**

$$
9x - 18 - 4x - 4 = 12
$$

**3. Combine like terms:**

$$
5x - 22 = 12
$$

**4. Isolate the variable term and solve:**

$$
5x = 34
$$

$$
x = \frac{34}{5}
$$

### Practice Problems

Solve for $$x$$ in each equation below.

1. $$4(2x-3) - 3(x+5) = 2(x-1)$$
2. $$\dfrac{2x-5}{3} + \dfrac{x+2}{2} = 4$$
3. $$5 - 2(3x-4) = 7x - (x+3)$$

### Solutions

**Problem 1**

$$4(2x - 3) - 3(x + 5) = 2(x - 1)$$

1. Expand expressions:

$$8x - 12 - 3x - 15 = 2x - 2$$

2. Combine like terms on the left side:

$$5x - 27 = 2x - 2$$

3. Isolate variable terms on one side:

$$3x = 25$$

4. Solve for $$x$$:

$$x = \frac{25}{3}$$

**Problem 2**

$$\frac{2x - 5}{3} + \frac{x + 2}{2} = 4$$

1. Clear fractions by multiplying every term by the LCD (6):

$$6 \cdot \left(\frac{2x - 5}{3}\right) + 6 \cdot \left(\frac{x + 2}{2}\right) = 6 \cdot 4$$

$$2(2x - 5) + 3(x + 2) = 24$$

2. Expand expressions:

$$4x - 10 + 3x + 6 = 24$$

3. Combine like terms:

$$7x - 4 = 24$$

4. Isolate the variable term and solve:

$$7x = 28$$

$$x = 4$$

**Problem 3**

$$5 - 2(3x - 4) = 7x - (x + 3)$$

1. Expand expressions (distribute negative signs carefully):

$$5 - 6x + 8 = 7x - x - 3$$

2. Combine like terms on both sides:

$$-6x + 13 = 6x - 3$$

3. Isolate variable terms on one side:

$$16 = 12x$$

4. Solve for $$x$$ and reduce to simplest form:

$$x = \frac{16}{12} = \frac{4}{3}$$

## Part 2: Expanding Expressions

### Core Identities & Execution Rules

Expanding algebraic expressions requires removing parentheses through distribution and consolidating like terms. Special product patterns accelerate computation and prevent sign errors:

- **Distributive Property:** $$a(b + c) = ab + ac$$
- **FOIL Method (Binomial Multiplication):** $$(a + b)(c + d) = ac + ad + bc + bd$$
- **Perfect Square Binomials:**
  - $$(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$$
  - $$(a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$$
- **Difference of Squares Product:** $$(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$$

### Worked Reference Example

Expand and simplify:

$$(2x - 3)^2 - (x + 4)(3x - 1)$$

1. Expand the perfect square term $$(2x - 3)^2$$ using the square rule:

$$(2x)^2 - 2(2x)(3) + 3^2 = 4x^2 - 12x + 9$$

2. Expand the binomial product $$(x + 4)(3x - 1)$$ using FOIL:

$$x(3x) + x(-1) + 4(3x) + 4(-1) = 3x^2 - x + 12x - 4 = 3x^2 + 11x - 4$$

3. Subtract the second expanded expression (distributing the negative sign):

$$(4x^2 - 12x + 9) - (3x^2 + 11x - 4) = 4x^2 - 12x + 9 - 3x^2 - 11x + 4$$

4. Combine like terms:

$$(4x^2 - 3x^2) + (-12x - 11x) + (9 + 4) = x^2 - 23x + 13$$

### Practice Problems

Expand and fully simplify each expression below.

1. $$(3x + 2)(2x - 5)$$
2. $$(4x - 3)^2$$
3. $$2x(x - 4) - (2x + 3)(2x - 3)$$

### Solutions

**Problem 1**

$$(3x + 2)(2x - 5)$$

1. Apply the FOIL method:

$$(3x)(2x) + (3x)(-5) + (2)(2x) + (2)(-5) = 6x^2 - 15x + 4x - 10$$

2. Combine like terms:

$$6x^2 - 11x - 10$$

**Problem 2**

$$(4x - 3)^2$$

1. Apply the perfect square rule $$(a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2ab + b^2$$ where $$a = 4x$$ and $$b = 3$$:

$$(4x)^2 - 2(4x)(3) + 3^2$$

2. Simplify terms:

$$16x^2 - 24x + 9$$

**Problem 3**

$$2x(x - 4) - (2x + 3)(2x - 3)$$

1. Expand $$2x(x - 4)$$ using distribution:

$$2x^2 - 8x$$

2. Expand $$(2x + 3)(2x - 3)$$ using the difference of squares product $$(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$$:

$$(2x)^2 - 3^2 = 4x^2 - 9$$

3. Subtract the second expanded term (distributing the negative sign):

$$(2x^2 - 8x) - (4x^2 - 9) = 2x^2 - 8x - 4x^2 + 9$$

4. Combine like terms:

$$-2x^2 - 8x + 9$$

## Part 3: Factoring Polynomials

### Core Factoring Techniques

Factoring breaks an expression into simpler expressions whose product yields the original polynomial. Execute factoring systematically using the following hierarchy:

1. **Greatest Common Factor (GCF):** always check for and extract the GCF first:

$$ab + ac = a(b + c)$$

2. **Difference of Squares Pattern:** check if the expression fits the form $$a^2 - b^2$$:

$$a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$$

3. **Trinomial Factoring** ($$x^2 + bx + c$$): find two numbers $$p$$ and $$q$$ that multiply to $$c$$ and add to $$b$$:

$$x^2 + bx + c = (x + p)(x + q)$$

4. **Trinomial Factoring via the AC Method** ($$ax^2 + bx + c$$ where $$a \neq 1$$):
   - Find two numbers $$p$$ and $$q$$ such that $$p \cdot q = a \cdot c$$ and $$p + q = b$$.
   - Rewrite the middle term $$bx$$ as $$px + qx$$.
   - Group terms in pairs and factor out common binomials.

### Worked Reference Example

Factor completely:

$$2x^3 - 8x^2 - 24x$$

1. Extract the Greatest Common Factor ($$2x$$):

$$2x(x^2 - 4x - 12)$$

2. Factor the inner quadratic $$x^2 - 4x - 12$$: identify two factors of $$-12$$ that sum to $$-4$$. The numbers are $$-6$$ and $$2$$ (since $$-6 \cdot 2 = -12$$ and $$-6 + 2 = -4$$).

3. Write the complete factored form:

$$2x(x - 6)(x + 2)$$

### Practice Problems

Factor each polynomial completely.

1. $$x^2 - 9x + 20$$
2. $$16x^2 - 25$$
3. $$3x^2 + 7x - 6$$

### Solutions

**Problem 1**

$$x^2 - 9x + 20$$

1. Identify parameters: trinomial with $$a = 1$$, $$b = -9$$, and $$c = 20$$.
2. Find factors of $$c$$: two numbers that multiply to $$20$$ and add up to $$-9$$. The numbers are $$-4$$ and $$-5$$ (since $$-4 \cdot -5 = 20$$ and $$-4 + (-5) = -9$$).
3. Write the factored form:

$$(x - 4)(x - 5)$$

**Problem 2**

$$16x^2 - 25$$

1. Identify structure: difference of squares pattern $$a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$$.
2. Identify $$a$$ and $$b$$: $$a = \sqrt{16x^2} = 4x$$, $$b = \sqrt{25} = 5$$.
3. Apply pattern:

$$(4x - 5)(4x + 5)$$

**Problem 3**

$$3x^2 + 7x - 6$$

1. Apply the AC method: $$a \cdot c = 3 \cdot (-6) = -18$$.
2. Find factor pairs: two numbers that multiply to $$-18$$ and add to $$7$$. The numbers are $$9$$ and $$-2$$ (since $$9 \cdot -2 = -18$$ and $$9 + (-2) = 7$$).
3. Rewrite the middle term ($$7x$$):

$$3x^2 + 9x - 2x - 6$$

4. Factor by grouping:

$$(3x^2 + 9x) - (2x + 6)$$

$$3x(x + 3) - 2(x + 3)$$

5. Extract the common binomial:

$$(3x - 2)(x + 3)$$
