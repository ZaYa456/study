# Prerequisites — 5-Week Master Plan

A targeted review of the foundational topics that higher-level math relies on.
Plan for 5–7 hours of study per week: 30% concept review, 70% active
problem-solving.

| Module | Focus Area | Core Skills | Mastery Verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Algebraic Operations** | Equations, expansion, factoring | Multi-step linear equations, FOIL, factoring quadratics ($$x^2 + bx + c$$, difference of squares, AC method) | Factor 10 quadratics of varying difficulty without errors or assistance. |
| **2. Exponents & Radicals** | Exponent laws, roots, fractional powers | Product/quotient/power rules, simplifying radicals ($$\sqrt{x}$$), handling fractional exponents ($$x^{a/b}$$) | Simplify 5 complex expressions involving both negative and fractional exponents. |
| **3. Functions & Graphing** | Notation, transformations, domains | Evaluating $$f(x)$$, finding domain/range, vertical/horizontal shifts of $$y = x^2$$, reading key features | Sketch a transformed parabola from its equation and correctly state domain/range. |
| **4. Rational Expressions** | Algebraic fractions | Multiplying/dividing rational terms, finding LCD to add/subtract, solving rational equations | Complete 3 multi-step rational equations and check for extraneous solutions. |
| **5. Basic Trigonometry** | Right triangles & unit circle | SOH CAH TOA, special right triangles ($$30°$$-$$60°$$-$$90°$$, $$45°$$-$$45°$$-$$90°$$), unit circle coordinates, $$\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$$ | Evaluate exact trigonometric values for key angles without a calculator. |

## Execution Framework

- **Active problem-solving:** complete a minimum of 15–20 practice problems per topic. Passive reading of worked examples creates false familiarity.
- **Error tracking:** maintain an error log for every incorrect answer. Classify errors as structural (misunderstanding a concept), algebraic (procedural mistake), or execution (arithmetic/sign error).
- **Targeted drill-down:** when stuck on a sub-step during a practice problem, pause to solve 5 dedicated exercises on that specific mechanic before continuing.
